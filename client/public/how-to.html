Dropship Easy · Multi-Order Processing for monday

How to use the app

Dropship Easy turns Shopify orders into monday records and automates the whole dropshipping flow confirmation, supplier and courier selection, shipments, and supplier manifests. Here's how to set it up and use it.

Getting started (one-time setup)

  1. Install the app. On install, Dropship Easy automatically creates all the boards and columns it needs you don't create anything manually.
  2. Connect your account. Open Account Settings and click Connect to authorize the app. This is required so order webhooks can write to your boards.
  3. Add your service credentials. In Account Settings, enter your WhatsApp and Shiprocket details so confirmations and shipping work.
  4. Connect Shopify. Copy the Shopify Order Webhook URL from Account Settings and paste it into Shopify → Settings → Notifications → Webhooks as an Order creation webhook (format JSON). Each account's URL is unique.
  5. You're ready. New Shopify orders now flow into your Orders and Order Line Items boards automatically.

Set up your products & suppliers Required

Do this once, after the boards and columns have been created and before you process any orders. It tells Dropship Easy which supplier can fulfil each product, so it can split orders and pick the right supplier automatically.

  1. Import or create your products. Add your catalogue to the Products board (import a spreadsheet or add items manually).
  2. Populate the Supplier Products board. This board is the link between a supplier and the products they sell.
  3. Create a group for each supplier. On the Supplier Products board, add one group per supplier so their products stay together.
  4. Add the products each supplier sells. Inside that supplier's group, add a record for every product that supplier can fulfil.
  5. Connect the records. On each Supplier Product record, use the Connected Board columns to link it to the corresponding Supplier and Product.
  6. Enter the Available Quantity. Set the quantity that supplier has available for each product.

Why this matters: when an order arrives, Dropship Easy uses these Supplier Product links to identify which supplier can fulfil each product. Without this setup, it can't match products to suppliers and orders can't be processed correctly.

Processing orders

Open the Multi Order Processing board view and work through four steps:

1

Order Selection

Confirmed orders are listed select the ones you want to process.

2

Supplier Selection

Assign a supplier to each line item. Orders with items from multiple suppliers are split into sub-orders, and the order is created in Shiprocket.

3

Courier Selection

The app checks Shiprocket serviceability; pick a courier for each shipment. Use Select best for all to auto-pick the recommended option.

4

Create Shipment & Manifest

Click Process Orders the app assigns AWBs, creates the shipments, and generates supplier manifests and shipping labels. Set Send Email To Supplier to Send to email the manifest.

A look at each step

Order confirmation (WhatsApp)

From the Orders board you can send a WhatsApp confirmation to the customer. Their reply (Confirm / Cancel) updates the order status automatically, so only confirmed orders move into processing.

WhatsApp setup: to set up WhatsApp for your account, please follow the How to use guide to create your WhatsApp message template, then enter your WhatsApp credentials in Account Settings.

Tracking & cancellation

Tracking: open an order item and use the Order Tracking view to see live shipment status, the delivery timeline, and courier details.

Cancel a shipment: on the Shipments board, set the Cancel Shipment status to Cancel — the app cancels it in Shiprocket and writes the result to the Cancellation Response column.

AI capabilities ✨ Powered by monday AI

Dropship Easy includes AI features powered by monday's own AI no data leaves your monday account, and there's no third-party AI to configure. These features need a monday Pro or Enterprise plan with AI enabled; if AI isn't available, the app falls back to its standard behavior.

✨ Board AI Assistant

A button in the board header opens an assistant that answers questions about the board you're on using a summary of that board's items and statuses. Ask things like:

  • "Summarize this board."
  • "How many orders are pending supplier selection?"
  • "Break down the items by status."
  • "What needs my attention?"

Works on any board (Orders, Order Line Items, Suppliers, Shipments, Error Logs, …) and uses the right terms for each. It sends only counts/statuses to the AI never customer names, addresses, or phone numbers.

✨ AI Rank Supplier & Courier

On the Supplier Selection and Courier Selection screens, the ✨ AI Rank button asks monday AI to reorder the options in each dropdown from best to worst and tag them Best / Good / Average / Poor, each with a short reason (shown with a ✨).

  • Suppliers are ranked from your Supplier Products and Suppliers boards (price, rating, available quantity).
  • Couriers are ranked from Shiprocket's live options for the route (freight, rating, delivery time, RTO charges).

AI Rank only re-sorts what's already in the dropdown it doesn't select, tick, or save anything for you. You still choose and click Update. If AI isn't available it keeps the standard ranking.
